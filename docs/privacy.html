DT Restora Demo - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

DT Restora Demo (package bd.com.datatechbd.dt_restora) is the staff companion app of the DT Restora restaurant management suite, published by Datatech BD.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your sign-in token, your preferences (theme, sounds, brand name and colour) and offline drafts of orders, payments, shifts, wastage and stock counts on this device only.

Data sent to a server

Order, payment and account data (such as name, email, phone and purchase history) is sent over HTTPS to the DT Restora server you configure in the app. That data is handled under the policies of the restaurant operating that server.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

Deletion

Signing out or deleting the app removes account data from the device. To have data on the server removed, contact the restaurant that operates it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
